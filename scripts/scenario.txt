THE CUSTOMER
RetailCo. 120 stores across APAC. 2,000 frontline staff.

Their COO raised four problems:
1. "I have no idea what actually happens on my shop floor. I get a spreadsheet on Monday about last week."
2. "My best store managers outsell my worst ones by 3x. I cannot bottle what they do."
3. "Onboarding a new hire takes six weeks and depends entirely on whoever happens to be working that day."
4. "Scheduling our store staff is a pain — getting one roster set takes coordination across many people and teams."

THE PRODUCTS WE ARE SELLING
- Spacesight — see what's happening on the floor (in-store vision analytics).
- Echo — hear what frontline staff actually say to customers (conversation capture and scoring).
- AI Wiki (Harbor Wiki) — bottle what the best people do so anyone can look it up.
- Alivia — turns all of the above into a client proposal.

THE TASK
Pitch these products back to RetailCo's COO as one joined-up proposal that solves the problems above.
